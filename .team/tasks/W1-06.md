- `core/scenario.py`:
  - `airport_limits` = destination's row (provenance `synthetic`).
  - `aar_nominal = round(max_runway_ops_per_hour × arrival_share_of_runway_ops)`, provenance `synthetic` (it's the organisers' synthetic limit now, no longer real-derived); `aar_reduced` = weather factor as before.

- `core/metrics.py`: `gate_change`, `gate_change_notice_min`, `fuel_at_landing_kg`; `ramp_overtime` costed × `ground_overtime_cost_multiplier` in `cost_J`.
