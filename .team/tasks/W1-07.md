## 1. Role tension (your gap: PILOT and GROUND both prefer the earliest option)
Proposal §4.1: GROUND protects gate validity, ramp overtime and the next departure's turnaround; PILOT protects fuel, turbulence and connections. A real trade-off exists when **the earliest arrival lands before a compatible gate is free**: arriving early means a gate change (needs ≥ 20 min notice) or waiting on the ramp, while a slightly later slot fits the planned gate. Within the workbook rules, model that honestly:
- Waiting for a gate: if no compatible gate is free at in-block time, the option's in-block waits for the earliest free gate (`gate_wait_min`, which adds to misconnects, crew duty and turnaround). Only add a metric if needed (additive `Metrics.gate_wait_min: int = 0`; tell MANAGER for the changelog).
- The planned gate's occupancy (seeded, synthetic) should free up somewhere inside the eta-shift grid on a good share of rows, so that GROUND's favourite is "+N min at the planned gate, no gate change" while PILOT's is the earliest one.
- Acceptance: on both hero rows and ≥ 3 of 5 dev rows, the three role favourites are not all the same and PILOT ≠ GROUND on ≥ 3 rows. Re-run `eval/pick_heroes.py` and re-export the top 2 if the ranking changes. Paste the table.
