## How you work
- Your first task is `.team/tasks/W1-01.md`, then `W1-02.md`, then `W1-03.md`. Go straight from one to the next. Don't wait for review.
- W2 (agents) and W3 (console) are coding against the fixtures right now and will switch to your real functions at integration (~13:39). **Keep your function signatures identical to CONTRACTS.md.**

- Every synthetic field must be listed in `Scenario.provenance` as `"synthetic"`.
- Unit-test the checker and fallback with `pytest` (in `tests/`).
- Report with `DONE`/`BLOCKED` lines in `.team/status/W1.md` (format in PROTOCOL.md), then tell the human one line.
- If `data/raw/flights.csv` isn't there yet, write `BLOCKED W1-01: no raw data` in your status file, and meanwhile build `core/types.py`, `core/config.py`, `core/log.py` and a synthetic in-memory test row so the rest of the pipeline can progress.
